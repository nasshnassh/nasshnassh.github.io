Popular Commands

Simple, powerful, and easy to use.

/play

/ban

Moderation command to keep your server safe from rule-breakers.

/help

Displays a full list of commands and how to use the bot effectively.

/warn

Issues a warning to a member and logs it for future moderation review.

/stats

Shows your server statistics including member count and activity.

Create interactive polls to gather feedback from your community.
